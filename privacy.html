PERRY FAMILY SURVIVOR DRAFT

Privacy

Last updated September 27, 2026

This family game is run by Benson Perry at survivordraft.bensonperry.com.

Your account

Clerk handles sign-in and account security. Google sign-in shares your name, email address, and profile picture. Apple sign-in shares your name and email address; if you choose Hide My Email, Apple supplies a private relay address. The draft uses this information to identify your account. Email sign-in is also available through Clerk.

Your league data

The game stores your account ID, league name, team name, selected castaways, saved drafts, submitted team, watched episode, and save times. It also records commissioner changes to league settings and results.

Your draft is saved in this browser. Choosing Save team stores your seven picks in your account. Watched progress is saved to your account when signed in, or on this browser otherwise. Other players see your submitted picks after the commissioner locks the draft. League members can see your league name, team name, and submission status before then. Benson can administer and export league data.

Services that make the game work

GitHub Pages hosts the website, Cloudflare stores and processes league data, and Clerk handles authentication. These services may process technical information such as IP addresses and browser details to deliver and secure the app. Cast photos load from CBS and fonts load from Google Fonts. The site uses browser storage and sign-in cookies; it has no advertising or additional analytics scripts.

Read the privacy policies for Clerk, Cloudflare, GitHub, Google, and Apple.

Automatic results

The server reads public Survivor results from the survivoR dataset on GitHub. It does not send your account, team, or watched progress to that source.

Keeping and removing data

Teams and results may be kept as a family season archive. To ask for a copy or removal of your league data, contact Benson through bensonperry.com or directly. Signing out does not delete your saved league data or browser drafts. You can clear this site's browser storage to remove local drafts and watched progress.
